BANAHIS HYMN- The Red and the white
by: Joseph L. Johnson

Dear Batangas is our highschool the school we love the best.

The school we justly honor ranks far above the rest.

It is there we get the "spirit" the spirit which is right.

For it makes of us defenders of the red and of the white.

We stand as one united to aid what'er is just.

We shrink not from stern duty for work is our main trust.

We seek not fruitless glory we wish but for the might.

To ever stand up nobly for the red and for the white.

We win athletic victories in the field and on the track.

Our ball team is a winner our girls they ne'er turn back.

The school is always with them in the thickest of the fight!

For we all possess the spirit of the red and of the white.

When in the midst of life stern battle we our glorious part shall play

Our highschool we'll remember though we be far away.

We will always stand to aid her dear Batangas is our might

For we still shall have the spirit of the red and of the white.

OUR MISSION

The BCIHS Library strives to provide relevant library resources and quality services to help teachers and students of the K to 12 curriculums to catalyze their educational growth.

OUR VISION

The BCIHS Library envisions becoming a dynamic and responsive center for the student's learning development with their successful and competent completers.

OUR GOAL

Be able to provide quality services and friendly-environment library to serve and support our students and teachers with effective library services and resources to achieve the essence of the reading and learning activities.

OBJECTIVES

  • Provide every learning area with opportunities for students to engage in individual, small-group, and large-group activities that strengthen intellectual growth, information literacy, and cultural and social development.
  • Provide, organize, and regularly update relevant print and non-print resources aligned with the K-12 curriculum, in coordination with the general advisers of each grade level.
  • Promote and deliver both formal and informal orientation on library services to students at the start of every school year.
  • Implement varied monthly library activities and programs, in collaboration with teachers, to support students' continuous learning development.
  • Strengthen linkages with the SDO Batangas City Library Hub by actively promoting and supporting its activities, programs, and events.
  • Provide advanced technological services that help students and teachers improve their skills in using computers, audiovisual equipment, and other technical materials during the school year.
